From idea to demo

We’ll help you get there.

No hackathon experience needed. Mentors will be available throughout the event to help you plan, build, and present.

1

Pick one problem

Talk to your team, identify who you are helping, and keep the first version focused.

2

Sketch before you code

Agree on a simple solution, divide tasks, and build the smallest useful prototype.

3

Ask a mentor

Get feedback on your idea, technical choices, user experience, or pitch.

4

Demo and present

Show what works, explain the impact, and share what you would build next.
